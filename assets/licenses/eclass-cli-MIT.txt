rycont/eclass-cli
https://github.com/rycont/eclass-cli

Rhythm's Cybercampus connector (src-tauri/src/archive/cybercampus/) ports the
eClass request flow and page parsing of eclass-cli to Rust. eclass-cli declares
the MIT license in its README and has no separate license file; its author
permitted this use under MIT terms. The ported code is distributed in Rhythm
under GPL-3.0-or-later, and this notice is retained as MIT requires.
